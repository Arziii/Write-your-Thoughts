## **Write Your Thoughts - AI Memory System** 

## **Vision** 

The AI memory system is the heart of Write Your Thoughts. 

The AI should behave like a professional editor that gradually learns the writer's formatting preferences, writing style, and story context without replacing the author's creativity. 

The AI exists to preserve and enhance, not overwrite. 

## **Core Principles** 

1. Formatting has the highest priority. 

2. Writer preferences must always be respected. 

3. Story consistency is more important than perfect grammar. 

4. Memory should be modular and isolated. 

5. The writer always has control. 

6. AI suggestions are never mandatory. 

## **Memory Hierarchy** 

Priority: 

```
Formatting
↓
Writing Preferences
↓
Book Knowledge
↓
Chapter Context
↓
General Grammar
```

Formatting always wins.

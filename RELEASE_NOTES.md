# Write Your Thoughts - Release Notes

## v1.0.0 (The Initial Release)

Welcome to Write Your Thoughts v1.0.0! This is our very first major release. We've built an application focused entirely on making your writing experience lightning-fast, rock-solid, and completely reliable. We've spent a lot of time polishing how the app talks to the cloud, ensuring your stories are always safe, and making sure the app never skips a beat—even if your internet connection does!

### 🚀 What's New & Improved

* **Smarter Cloud Syncing:** The app is now incredibly smart about when to download your backups. When you log in, it instantly compares your local files with your cloud backup. It will only download data if your cloud backup is completely up-to-date and newer, guaranteeing that your recent offline writing is never accidentally overwritten!
* **Seamless Login Experience:** We've completely eliminated the awkward double-loading screens and UI flickering when you log in. Everything now syncs beautifully in the background while you log in, dropping you straight into your fully loaded workspace.
* **Forgot Password? No Problem!:** We've added a brand-new, polished "Forgot Password" and "Reset Password" flow. The app now fully supports clicking email links that automatically open the software and let you reset your password securely.
* **Smarter Routing:** When you manually sign in, the app now perfectly drops you into your Dashboard every single time, giving you a clean slate to choose which book you want to jump into.

### 🐛 Bug Fixes

* **Fixed the "Stuck on Login" Bug:** We squashed a rare bug where logging in with a slow internet connection could sometimes cause the app to freeze while trying to sync your books. 
* **Fixed Dashboard Cover Uploads:** Trying to upload a beautiful custom cover for your Dashboard? Sometimes the app would fail to upload the image, leaving you with a broken picture. This is now completely fixed—your images will upload securely and perfectly every time!
* **Cleaner Window Titles:** The top window bar will now cleanly display "Write Your Thoughts" when you are browsing your Dashboard, instead of accidentally displaying the title of the last chapter you were writing.

Thank you for choosing Write Your Thoughts to build your worlds. Happy writing!

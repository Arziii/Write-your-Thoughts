# Introduction

Welcome to the documentation for **Write Your Thoughts**!

## Vision

**Write Your Thoughts** is a desktop-first writing software designed explicitly for novelists, combining the familiarity of standard word processors with intelligent AI assistance and seamless cloud synchronization.

Our core philosophy revolves around empowering the author:

1. **The writer creates.**
2. **The AI refines.**
3. **The original work is always preserved.**

The AI's role is not to replace the author or generate the story, but to act as a professional developmental editor, continuity checker, and worldbuilding assistant that preserves the writer's ideas while improving clarity, grammar, flow, and structural integrity. The writer retains 100% control over the narrative.

## Target Audience

### Primary Users
- Solo writers
- Novelists
- Hobbyist writers
- Worldbuilders

### Secondary Users
- Script writers
- Students
- Bloggers

## Core Principles

- **Writer First**: AI suggestions are entirely optional. The writer has complete control.
- **Preserve Original Work**: AI must never silently overwrite drafts. AI polishing outputs into separate suggestions or versions.
- **Familiar Experience**: The rich text editor should feel similar to Microsoft Word or Google Docs, eliminating any steep learning curve.
- **Offline Reliability**: Writers can continue working entirely without an internet connection. AI functions require internet access, but writing and local saving do not.

## Platform

- **Desktop Application (Windows)**: Designed as an installed desktop app for maximum performance and local storage control.
- **Cloud Synchronization**: Background synchronization with Supabase allows you to access your manuscripts across multiple devices using account login.

## Chapter System

Unlike traditional word processors that use a single continuous document, manuscripts in **Write Your Thoughts** are broken down into discrete chapters. 

**Advantages:**
- Better organizational structure
- Easier backup and version control
- Improved AI context retrieval (the AI can fetch specific chapters instead of parsing an entire 100k-word manuscript)
- Independent, isolated editing sessions

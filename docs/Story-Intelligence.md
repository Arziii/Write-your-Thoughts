# Story Intelligence

**Story Intelligence** (formerly referred to as StoryMind) is the cognitive layer of Write Your Thoughts. Its purpose is to transform the application from a simple text editor into a professional developmental editor that deeply understands your specific manuscript.

## Core Philosophy

1. **Author Ownership**: The AI exists to enhance, not replace.
2. **Character Consistency**: Emotional and character continuity is sacred.
3. **Preservation**: The original draft is never overwritten silently.
4. **Context First**: Intelligence systems share memory and knowledge.

## Current Capabilities

### Workspace-Aware Brainstorming
The AI Assistant panel automatically gathers context about your currently open Book, Chapter, and Selected Text. When you ask questions like "How should she react to this?", the AI does not need you to paste the previous paragraphs; it dynamically reads your workspace to infer the subject.

### Timeline Analysis
By leveraging the AI to read an entire sequence of chapters, the Timeline Analyzer can automatically extract significant events, deduce their sequential story-day occurrence, and populate a visual timeline. 

### Continuity & Style Intelligence (Work in Progress)
The architecture supports defining a "Baseline Style" for a book (measuring prose density, vocabulary complexity, and dialogue ratios). Future iterations will use this baseline to warn authors if a newly written chapter drastically deviates from their established voice.

## The Story Bible Integration

Story Intelligence relies entirely on the user's populated worldbuilding nodes:
- Character Profiles
- Location Geography
- Organizational Hierarchies
- Magic Systems & World Rules

When analyzing continuity, the AI references these nodes as the ultimate source of truth to flag contradictions (e.g., "You established in Rule #4 that magic requires water, but the protagonist just cast a spell in a desert without a source").

## Future Roadmap

- **Relationship Graphs**: Visualizing character affinities based on sentiment analysis of chapters.
- **Voice Analyzers**: Ensuring dialogue strings match the assigned character's historical speech patterns.

## **Write Your Thoughts** 

## **Vision** 

**Write Your Thoughts** is a desktop-first writing software with cloud synchronization and AI-assisted editing. It is designed to function as a personal writing environment for novelists, combining the familiarity of Microsoft Word with AI memory and editing capabilities. 

The AI's role is not to replace the author, but to act as an editor and writing assistant that preserves the writer's ideas while improving clarity, grammar, flow, and readability. 

The writer retains 100% control over the AI, and the original draft is never overwritten. 

## **Philosophy** 

**The writer creates.** 

**The AI refines.** 

**The original work is always preserved.**
